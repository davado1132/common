All About Nemo

The adventurous clownfish from Pixar's Finding Nemo.

Who is Nemo?

Nemo is a young clownfish who lives in the Great Barrier Reef. His journey begins when he's captured by a diver and placed in a fish tank, prompting his father Marlin to cross the ocean in search of him.

Fun Facts

  • Nemo was voiced by Alexander Gould.
  • The film Finding Nemo won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2004.
  • Clownfish live among sea anemones, which protect them from predators.
